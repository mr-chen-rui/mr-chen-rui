What is inspiration?

Inspiration is when you hear about something and think, "Hey, that's cool! I wanna make that!!". For instance, the background of this website was inspired by the background of the official WWSEF website; however, I still coded my own website's background from scratch.
Some people might try to tell you that inspiration is like stealing. Don't listen to them. Remember that all great discoveries are built off of other discoveries.
As long as you learn something from it, inspiration is not stealing.

Where do I get my inspiration?

I get my inspiration from a multitude of different sources.
Oftentimes, I get my inspiration from books that I read (The Martian by Andy Weir was especially great), things that I see, and people I talk to. Anything can act as inspiration, which is why you should always keep an open mind. For example, the massive panorama you saw on the main page was directly inspired by the webcomic XKCD by Randall Munroe.
