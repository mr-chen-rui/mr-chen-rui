Who Am I?

I'm a person who does 3D modelling, codeing, and fiddling around with electronics in his free time! I also have a 3D printer (Ender 3 V2), and know multiple languages, namely C++, English, Python, French, Java, Chinese, and C#. I make really cool projects (this website is one of them), and often post them online.
This website was not made from a template, and is made purely with vanilla Javascript, HTML, and CSS.

Why do I code?

I code not for money, but because I want to learn how things are made. If I were to use LLMs to code, I would learn next to nothing, and I would be no better off.
Similarly, I refuse to use AI to generate textures or 3D models, and I model everything that I 3D print myself.
